How To Make Orange Juice

Scroll Down To Follow The Steps

Step 1

Gather your ingredients: fresh oranges, a knife, a cutting board, and a juicer or blender.

Choose the Oranges

Begin by selecting fresh, ripe oranges for the best flavor. Wash them carefully before preparing the juice.

Step 2

Cut the oranges in half using a knife and a cutting board. Make sure to remove any seeds if present.

Step 3

Squeeze the juice from the oranges.

Squeeze the Juice

Use a juicer or blender to extract the juice from the orange halves. If using a blender, you may need to strain the juice to remove pulp.

"

Step 4

Pour the juice into a glass and enjoy your fresh homemade orange juice!

Your Orange Juice Is Ready!

Enjoy your fresh, healthy orange juice!
